# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 30, 2026  
**Product:** VRCFX (Windows desktop app)

This Privacy Policy explains what VRCFX collects, what it does not collect, and how your information is handled.

---

## Summary

VRCFX is built to run on **your computer**.  
Most data stays **local**. VRCFX talks to **VRChat** so the app can work, and may check **GitHub Releases** when you ask for updates. VRCFX does **not** sell your data and does **not** run ads.

---

## What VRCFX uses

### 1. VRChat account session
When you sign in, VRCFX uses your VRChat credentials (and 2FA if required) to create a session with VRChat’s servers.

- Session cookies / auth tokens are stored **locally** on your PC so you can stay signed in.
- VRCFX does not create a separate VRCFX cloud account.

### 2. VRChat data you already have access to
Depending on features you use, VRCFX may request from VRChat things like:

- your profile and avatar info  
- friends list and online / world status  
- favorite avatars and worlds  
- public world search results  

This is used only to show and operate those features inside the app.

### 3. Local VRChat log files (Live Radar)
If Live Radar / log watching is enabled, VRCFX may read VRChat’s local `output_log` files on your PC to detect the current instance and nearby players.

- Those files never leave your machine through VRCFX for “cloud analytics.”
- You can turn this off in Settings.

### 4. Local app data
VRCFX may store on your device:

- settings and preferences  
- friend notes / nicknames  
- activity history and visited instances  
- optional backups you export  

This data is stored in your Windows user app data folder unless you export it yourself.

### 5. Updates
If you use **Update**, VRCFX may contact GitHub Releases to check for a newer installer and download it.

---

## What VRCFX does not do

- Does **not** sell personal data  
- Does **not** show advertising trackers  
- Does **not** upload your friend notes or local history to a VRCFX cloud service  
- Does **not** read your VRChat password after login beyond what’s needed to authenticate with VRChat  

---

## Third parties

| Service | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **VRChat** | Login, friends, avatars, worlds, and related API features |
| **GitHub Releases** | Optional update checks / installer download |
| **Windows / your PC** | Local storage, notifications, optional startup |

VRChat’s and GitHub’s own policies also apply when those services are used.

---

## Your choices

- Sign out anytime to clear the active session path in the app.  
- Disable Live Radar / log watching in Settings.  
- Disable notifications or mute alerts.  
- Export or clear local backup data using the tools in Settings.  
- Uninstall VRCFX; optional app data can remain until you delete it (uninstall is configured not to wipe notes by default).

---

## Children’s privacy

VRCFX is intended for users who already use VRChat and can manage a VRChat account. It is not directed at children under 13.
