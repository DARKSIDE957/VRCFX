# VRCFX

**All-in-one VRChat companion for Windows.**

Stay connected to friends, switch avatars faster, explore worlds, and keep your VRChat life organized — without fighting the game for focus.

---

## What VRCFX Does

- **Friends** — live online status, world changes, and clean-up tools for inactive contacts  
- **Avatars** — wear favorites, manage lists, and keep your equipped look in sync  
- **Worlds** — browse saved worlds and explore what’s trending  
- **Live Radar** — see who’s in your current instance from local VRChat logs  
- **Alerts** — in-app and desktop notifications that stay out of your way  
- **Chatbox tools** — optional OSC helpers when you want them  

---

## Getting Started

1. Install VRCFX with the Windows installer from the latest release.  
2. Sign in with your VRChat account (2FA supported).  
3. Use the sidebar to jump between Friends, Worlds, Avatars, Radar, and Settings.  

Your notes, settings, and local history stay on your machine across updates.

---

## Updates

Use the **Update** button in the sidebar (above Mute).  
VRCFX checks the private release channel, downloads the installer when something newer is available, closes itself so the install can finish, then opens again when the installer completes.

---

## Privacy

VRCFX is distributed from a **private** repository.  
Access is limited to people you share it with. Session data and companion settings stay local unless you choose to back them up.

---

## Support

If something breaks, note what you were doing and which version you’re on (visible in the title bar / about area), then reach out to the person who shared VRCFX with you.
